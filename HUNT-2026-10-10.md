### 1. `warmGroundFloor` contending calls (silent drop → queue)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Finding** | While one ellipsoidal floor batch was in flight, a second `warmGroundFloor` poll used to return without scheduling its cells (`src/services/groundFloor.js`, Round 6 comment). Flights (30 s) and military (15 s) could lose polls for minutes at a new airport. |

| **Test** | `src/services/groundFloor.warm.test.mjs` — blocks the first `resolveEllipsoidalGround`, fires a second `warmGroundFloor` mid-flight, asserts a second batch runs for the queued cell. |
